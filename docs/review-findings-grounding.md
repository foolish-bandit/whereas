# Playbook-Grounded Review Findings

Review findings are workflow aids for legal and business teams. They are **not legal advice**.

## Grounding requirements

Whereas findings should only be produced when grounded in available evidence:

- Source text span and/or source excerpt from the contract text.
- Clause and metadata evidence when available.
- A playbook rule reference and/or playbook basis.
- Clause Manager fallback or approved language where available.
- Human review status for final disposition.

Ungrounded AI-generated findings must not be treated as actionable.

## Role of small models

Small generative models may explain an already-grounded finding in plain language, but they must not invent findings or policy basis.

## Human review

All findings require a human-in-the-loop review decision (`unreviewed`, `accepted`, `rejected`, `needs_revision`) before operational use.
